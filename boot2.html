Safety Points for Metro Subway System

This is a webpage where you will know the do's and dont's when travelling with a Metro.

Do's

  • Stand behind the yellow line on the platform.
  • Keep your belongings close to you.
  • Follow the instructions of metro staff.

Dont's

  • Do not run on the platform.
  • Avoid leaning on the train doors.
  • Do not block the doors while boarding or alighting.

General information

India's metro systems are designed with advanced safety mechanisms that comply with international urban transport standards.
